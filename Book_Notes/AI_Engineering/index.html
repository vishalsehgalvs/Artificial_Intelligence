Scope and Coverage

These notes explain the book's core concepts, algorithms, decision frameworks, formulas, trade-offs, failure modes, and representative examples in original wording. Every contents-page section is mapped in coverage.md. A section mapping confirms topic inclusion, not that every sentence or figure has been reproduced. Consult the supplied book for complete experiments, source code, citations, and finer detail.

The preface frames the book as a practical, start-simple framework, not a catalog of permanently current APIs. The epilogue emphasizes learning and building responsibly as the field changes. The bibliography and back-of-book index are lookup material rather than separate technical chapters.

The existing lecture notes remain unchanged. Each new HTML is self-contained with embedded styling and works offline; PDFs contain the corresponding chapter content.
